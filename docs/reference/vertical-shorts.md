PRISM Vertical Shorts

Procedure: cutting a long episode into 9:16 shorts

Vertical Shorts cuts a long-form programme into stand-alone 9:16 clips. The AI Vision model rates the picture, the dialogue is transcribed, and a story model proposes stretches that stand on their own. You review, adjust and render.

Note: the screenshots are from the real PRISM screens, using a synthetic sample episode and mocked AI services, so the moments and scores are illustrative.

1\. Overview

1.  Sign in (section 2) and create or open a project for the programme (section 3).

2.  Source: pick the video, set length, and analyse.

3.  Moments: review, trim, reframe and caption each short.

4.  Render, then play and download from "Shorts in this project".

2\. Sign in to PRISM

1.  Open PRISM in your browser at the address your administrator gave you.

2.  If the Sign in box does not appear, press "Log in" at the top right.

3.  Type your Username and Password (the credentials issued to you by your PRISM administrator), then press Continue. If your account has two-step verification, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or a backup code, on the next screen.

4.  No account yet? Press "Request access". An administrator must approve it before you can sign in. After too many wrong attempts the form pauses for a few minutes.

*Figure 1. Sign in box (see steps 1 to 4)*

3\. Open or create a project

1.  Press Vertical Shorts in the left menu.

2.  Each programme or episode is a project; its cuts, settings and rendered shorts stay inside it. Use "New project" (title, episode, air date, group), or open an existing one. Press All projects to come back to this list.

*Figure 2. Vertical Shorts project list (see steps 1 and 2)*

4\. Source screen

1.  Choose the long-form video with Browse library. Add optional captions (.srt/.vtt/.scc) if you have them.

2.  If the episode has several files, press "This episode comes in more than one part".

3.  Set "What to make", the length of each short (for example 20 to 40 s), the number of moments to find, and the spoken language (Auto-detect for mixed Filipino and English).

4.  Press Analyse. A queue card shows progress. When it says "Ready to review" the Moments screen opens.

*Figure 3. Source screen: video, length, moments and language (see steps 1 to 4)*

Earlier cuts of this project are listed at the top. Press Open to return to one without re-analysing; they are kept 14 days after last opened.

5\. More options

Open "More options" to steer the result.

- Feature / avoid: say what to prefer or keep out of the shorts.

- Names and terms: character and place names, so the captions spell them right.

- Leave out of every short: billboards, sponsor reads, credits, narration, host talk, recaps, teasers, black, logos, title cards, and talking heads. All are ticked by default; untick one to allow it.

- Leave out these parts: time ranges such as 0:00-1:45, 22:10-23:30 that no moment may include.

- Advanced: choose the AI vision model, story model, and frames to rate.

*Figure 4. More options*

6\. Output settings

Output settings holds the defaults for the batch. They are saved to the project, and you can save a set as a template.

- Framing: Auto reframes two-shots by splitting the screen, else follows the subject. "Follow the speaker" tracks who talks.

- Cuts: how pieces join (short crossfade by default).

- Captions: burn in, size, position over a split screen (on the join between the panes or low), and Word highlighting.

- EP labels: opening label (for example "EP {n}") and closing label (for example "NEXT: EP {n} \>\>"). Type any wording; {n} is the number.

- Delivery: file format and audio level (for example -14 LUFS for online video).

- Ending: the last short can fade to black, so the cliffhanger is not cut or held.

*Figure 5. Output settings panel*

7\. Moments screen

The left list holds the proposed shorts with a score. Use "Add your own moment" for a cut the AI missed.

*Figure 6. Moments list, player and Trim tab*

1.  Untick a moment in the left list to leave it out. Select one to preview it in the 9:16 player.

*Figure 7. Moments list: tick, untick and select*

2.  Trim tab: type In and Out, or use the -1, -0.1, +0.1, +1 buttons; in the player use 1 frame, Set In here, Set Out here and Cut from here. The title can be edited. Use Re-analyse this moment to ask the AI again.

8\. Framing

1.  Open the Framing tab. Each shot of the moment shows a dashed crop box.

2.  For each shot choose As worked out, Centre crop, Crop here (slide the box), Whole frame, or Split screen (two people stacked).

3.  Press "Show in the preview" to see the result, captions and all, in the 9:16 player. The 9:16 preview does not autoplay; press play when ready.

9\. Captions and dialogue

The Captions tab edits the caption text and timing of the burned-in subtitles. The Dialogue tab shows the transcript lines for the moment.

10\. Render

1.  Check the footer: ticked moments, total running time against the source.

2.  Leave "Replace the earlier render of a moment" ticked to re-render a single short in place.

3.  Press Render N shorts. The queue shows each short as it encodes. A light preview copy is made for smooth playback over Wi-Fi or VPN.

11\. Results

When finished, press "Show the shorts", or open the "Shorts in this project" tab. Each short shows its title, length, size and shot treatment. Click to play; download MP4 or SRT, edit Captions, or use "Download all (.zip)".

12\. Tips

- Re-rendering one short keeps its closing label and cliffhanger.

- Mini-drama chapters can end shorter than the minimum; the leftover at the end of a stretch is not padded.

- If something unwanted appears (a billboard, a host shot), add its time range under "Leave out these parts" and re-analyse.

- Settings are saved per project and restored on reload.

## Creating a new project (what to fill in)
Press Vertical Shorts, then New project. Only the Programme title is required.
- Programme title (required): the show name, for example Tadhana.
- Episode: the episode number, for example Ep. 101. Episode title: for example Ang Pagbabalik.
- Artist/s: optional, cast separated by commas. Air date: when the episode airs (defaults to today).
- Status: New for a project just started. Assigned to: the responsible team member, or Unassigned. Group: shows your own group.
- Has a deadline: tick it to enter a due date. Thumbnail: optional JPEG, PNG or WebP; without one the project shows a frame from its newest short. Description: optional notes.
- Press Create project (or Cancel). The project page opens on the Source screen. Edit details and Delete project are at the top of the project page.

## Extra facts
- Format choices in Output settings > Delivery include MP4 (H.264 High Profile), TikTok upload (MP4 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, up to 12 Mb/s), and Apple ProRes 422 HQ (29.97 or 23.976 fps, 1080x1920 .mov made with ffmpeg's prores_ks). Each short is also rendered as an MP4 for playing in the browser.
- For a target of 90 s, set the length range around it (for example 80 to 100), not one number. Mini-drama chapters can come out shorter than the minimum because the leftover at the end of a stretch is not padded.
- "Fade the last short to black" is ticked by default and fades the final short of the render, which removes a cliffhanger on that short; untick it unless it is the last episode of a series.
- Browse library can offer several source folders per category: add them under Config > Network > More sources, then pick from the Source dropdown.

## Using your own list of moments (CSV)

On the Source screen, "Use my list of moments (optional CSV)" lets the editor decide the moments instead of the AI.

- File: CSV (comma, semicolon or tab). First row names the columns: `title`, `in`, `out`, and optionally `part` and `note`. Without a header row the columns are `title,in,out` (or just `in,out`). Up to 60 rows.
- Times: seconds (`83.5`), `1:23.5`, `0:01:23.5`, or frames `00:01:23:15` (at the video's frame rate).
- Several parts: put the part number in a `part` column and give times in that part's own clock. Without a `part` column, times are on the joined timeline. A moment past the end of its video or part is cut there.
- What happens: vision rating and the story model are skipped (so no Ollama needed); the rows become the moments on the Moments screen, with scores blank. In and out are used as given (put on whole frames). Captions still come from the supplied caption files or from speech-to-text. Trim, framing, captions, EP labels, cliffhanger, output settings and render work as normal.
- Rows that cannot be used (bad time, out before in, longer than the 300-second limit, no such part) are skipped and listed in the warnings.

## Standard procedure uses Mini-drama

The Word procedure (PRISM_Vertical_Shorts_Procedure.docx) is written for the Mini-drama selection ("What to make" = Mini-drama): the whole episode is cut into consecutive chapters (Ep 1, Ep 2 ...), none skipped, with EP opening and NEXT closing labels. Moments to find, Feature and Avoid do not apply to it. Best moments (AI picks) and Highlight reel are the other selections. A CSV moments list overrides the selection.
